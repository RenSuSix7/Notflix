Jujutsu Kaisen (2018)

A high schooler Yuji Itadori, who joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers after becoming the host of Ryomen Sukuna, a powerful, evil Curse. To prevent widespread destruction, Yuji consumes all of Sukuna's fingers, aiming to eventually destroy himself and the curse. The story highlights themes of sacrifice, moral ambiguity, and intense supernatural battles in a world plagued by curses.
